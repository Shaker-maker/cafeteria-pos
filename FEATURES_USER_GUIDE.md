# Quick Start Guide - New Features

## 🎯 Item Availability Management

### Problem It Solves
"Our items run out during the day, but customers still see them in the menu"

### Solution
Mark items as OUT OF STOCK so they disappear from customer's menu automatically

### Steps

**Step 1:** Open Settings
- Go to ⚙️ **Settings** tab (bottom tab bar)

**Step 2:** Find "Manage Menu" 
- Scroll down to **Manage Menu** section
- You'll see all your menu items listed

**Step 3:** Toggle Item Status

For each item, you can see:
```
[Item Name]
KES [Price]
[Status: ✅ Available or ❌ Out of Stock]
```

**Option A: Mark Item as OUT OF STOCK**
- Click the **"↓ Mark Out"** button next to item
- Item turns gray with strikethrough
- Shows "❌ Out of Stock"
- Disappears from customer menu immediately

**Option B: Restore Item to Available**
- Item was marked out of stock?
- Click the **"↑ Restore"** button
- Item becomes available again
- Reappears in customer menu immediately

### Real Example

```
Morning: You have 10 Mandazis
↓ Customers order some
↓ You run out around 2 PM
↓ Click "↓ Mark Out" on Mandazi
↓ Students from 2 PM onwards won't see Mandazi in menu
↓ 3 PM: You make more Mandazis
↓ Click "↑ Restore" on Mandazi
↓ Menu updated! Students can order again
```

## 📄 Digital Receipts

### Problem It Solves
"We need receipts for accountability, especially for cash payments"

### Solution
Automatic receipt generation after every sale - print or email!

### What Happens

**During Sale:**
```
[Normal Sale Process]
↓ Select items
↓ Enter student name
↓ Select payment method
↓ Click "Complete Sale"
↓ [NEW] Receipt appears automatically! 🎉
```

**Receipt Shows:**
- Business name (Chafua POS)
- Receipt number (RCP-1-2024...)
- Date and time
- Student name (if entered)
- All items purchased (with quantities)
- Total amount
- Payment method (M-Pesa / Cash)

### What You Can Do With Receipt

#### 🖨️ PRINT
1. Click **"🖨️ Print"** button
2. Your phone's print dialog opens
3. Options:
   - Print to physical printer (if available)
   - Save as PDF to phone
   - Share with others

#### 📧 EMAIL
1. Click **"📧 Email"** button
2. Enter customer's email address
3. Receipt sent to their email
4. They have digital copy for their records

#### ✅ DONE
- Close modal when done
- Cart automatically clears
- Ready for next customer

### Real Workflow

```
2:30 PM - John orders
├─ Adds: Chapati (x2), Tea (x1)
├─ Student name: John Omondi
├─ Payment: Cash
├─ Clicks "Complete Sale"
│
└─ Receipt appears:
   ├─ Receipt #: RCP-1-456
   ├─ Date: 2/3/2026 2:30 PM
   ├─ Items:
   │  ├─ Chapati x2 = KES 100
   │  └─ Tea x1 = KES 50
   ├─ Total: KES 150
   ├─ Payment: CASH
   │
   ├─ Vendor options:
   │  ├─ 🖨️ Print (to printer or PDF)
   │  ├─ 📧 Email (to John: john@example.com)
   │  └─ ✅ Done (next sale)
```

### Why It Matters

✅ **Accountability** - Every transaction has a record  
✅ **For Audits** - Easy to track all sales  
✅ **Customer Happy** - They get receipt proof  
✅ **Professional** - Shows business is organized  
✅ **Digital Record** - Can email instead of print

## 💡 Pro Tips

### Item Availability
- **Daily Routine:** Check at opening to set available items
- **During Day:** Mark sold-out items immediately as "Out of Stock"
- **Batch Updates:** Do it quickly in Settings tab
- **Customer Experience:** Empty menu is better than "sorry, we're out"

### Receipts
- **Cash Customers:** Always print or email receipt for proof
- **M-Pesa:** Email receipt so they have it on file
- **End of Day:** You have digital records of all sales
- **Busy Times:** Can skip email and just print for speed

## ❓ FAQ

**Q: Can customers see the receipt?**
A: Not directly. You print or email it to them.

**Q: What if I need a receipt later?**
A: It's stored in the system. Go to Sales tab and find the transaction.

**Q: Does email work automatically?**
A: Currently shows confirmation. Full email integration coming soon.

**Q: What if item is marked out but we get more stock?**
A: Click "↑ Restore" to immediately make it available again.

**Q: Does availability change affect previous sales data?**
A: No! Only affects what's shown going forward.

**Q: Can I undo "Mark Out"?**
A: Yes! Click "↑ Restore" to undo immediately.
