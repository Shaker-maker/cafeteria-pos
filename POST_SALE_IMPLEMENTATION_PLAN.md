# Post-Sale Implementation Plan

## If They Say "YES" Tomorrow - Here's Your Roadmap

---

## 🎯 Phase 1: IMMEDIATE (Day 1 - First Day at Cafeteria)

### What You Need to Bring
- [ ] Laptop/backup laptop
- [ ] 2-3 tablets/phones for staff
- [ ] USB charger cables
- [ ] Wi-Fi router (if needed)
- [ ] Printed menu items list
- [ ] Contract/receipt

### Step 1: Physical Setup (30 mins)
1. **Choose location for ordering station**
   - Should be visible to staff
   - Good Wi-Fi signal
   - Power outlet nearby

2. **Deploy to their hardware**
   - Install app on tablets/phones for staff
   - Test internet connection
   - Set up Wi-Fi credentials

3. **Set up database**
   ```
   - Create unique business PIN (NOT 0000)
   - Create their business account
   - Test first transaction
   ```

### Step 2: Menu Configuration (45 mins - 1 hour)
1. **Add their menu items** (you do this with them watching)
   - Go to Settings ⚙️
   - Click "Manage Menu"
   - Add items from their menu one by one
   
2. **Get their prices** (critical!)
   - Ask: "What's the price for each item?"
   - Write down/take photos
   - Enter exact prices in app

3. **Verify items appear**
   - Go to Sell tab
   - Show items organized by category
   - Confirm formatting looks good

**Sample Menu to Add:**
```
🍔 BURGERS
- Beef Burger ................. 150 KES
- Chicken Burger .............. 120 KES

🍟 SIDES
- French Fries ................ 80 KES

🥤 DRINKS
- Soda ........................ 50 KES
- Water ....................... 30 KES

(... continue with their items)
```

### Step 3: Staff Training (1 hour)
**Gather 2-3 staff members** who will use the system

**Teach them:**
1. **How to Log In**
   - Show PIN entry
   - Explain security

2. **How to Take Orders**
   - Customer walks up
   - Staff selects items by tapping
   - Taps customer phone number (or "Walk-in")
   - Taps "Proceed to Payment"

3. **Payment Methods**
   - Show M-Pesa option
   - Show Cash option
   - Explain receipt

4. **Corrections**
   - How to remove items from cart
   - How to change quantity

5. **End of Shift**
   - Show them the reports
   - Show how to view daily sales

**Do 5-10 Practice Transactions**
- Have them ring up fake orders
- Let them process payments (use test M-Pesa)
- Build confidence

## 🚨 POTENTIAL ISSUES & SOLUTIONS

### Issue: Wi-Fi Drops
**Solution:** 
- Show them offline mode works
- Continue taking orders
- Sync when internet returns
- Not a show-stopper

### Issue: Staff Resistance
**Solution:**
- More training
- Show them time saved
- Make it easier than before
- Takes 2-3 days to adjust

### Issue: Slow Payments
**Solution:**
- Could be M-Pesa API issue
- Have fallback to manual payment
- Investigate with Safaricom
- Alternative: Ask for payment upfront, complete order after

### Issue: Wrong Prices
**Solution:**
- Easy fix - delete and re-add item
- Takes 1 minute
- Do it while they watch
